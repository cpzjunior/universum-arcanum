# Universum Arcanum

## Overview

Universum Arcanum is a fictional worldbuilding project in the dark fantasy genre, primarily inspired by a darker interpretation of Harry Potter and Forgotten Realms. From the first reference, it draws upon the coexistence of magical and non-magical societies, exploring a reality in which wizards wield power, influence, and privileges over the non-magical population. From the second, it incorporates the breadth of a fantasy setting populated by different cultures, civilizations, creatures, and territories, without adopting a structure of numerous pantheons or excessively extensive lore. The goal is to build a universe rich in possibilities, yet grounded in clear foundations and manageable complexity.

The project's guiding principle is immersion. To achieve this, the documents that make up the universe are presented as works produced by its own inhabitants, situated within a particular historical and cultural context. A treatise written by an archmage and historian, for example, reflects its author's knowledge and perspective, not an omniscient view of reality. Thus, the universe is not explained through an external voice, but gradually revealed through its records, traditions, and interpretations.

## Main Characteristics

The cosmology begins with the Logos, understood as the totality of Existence, encompassing multiple Dominium, each with its own identity, cosmological structure, natural laws, and history. Arcania is the primary domain developed in the project, but it does not represent the entirety of the Logos. Faeris, Ersetu, Aetheris, Cristalis, Nereia, Erebos, and Stygia are some of the other known Dominium.

The cosmological structure distinguishes between Existence, essence, and manifestation. The Dominium are classified as Mortalis and Immortalis according to the presence of established immortal entities. The Immortalis receive an additional classification: Superioris, Inferioris, or Equilibris, according to the predominant orientation of these entities.

Magic is a discipline of knowledge and transformation of reality, founded on principles such as Harmony, Transformation, Causality, Responsible Freedom, and Inner Flame. Although it exerts a decisive influence on the organization of societies, it is a rare ability: only 1% of the human population manifests magical aptitude. This concentration contributes to social structures in which wizards hold privileges, institutional influence, and positions of power inaccessible to most of the population.

Arcane knowledge is preserved and transmitted through Arcane Schools and Families, organizations structured around their own lineages, traditions, and philosophies. Each school has its own training methods, techniques, and interpretations of magic. Bonds of belonging, reputation, rivalries, alliances, and succession disputes influence the evolution of these traditions and the distribution of magical knowledge.

Geography also exerts a direct influence on magic. Different territories possess their own affinities, determined by their natural, historical, and supernatural characteristics. These properties can enhance, limit, or modify arcane manifestations. The recurrent use of magic can leave behind energetic residues, cause anomalies, and permanently alter the environment, turning certain regions into strategic resources contested by families, organizations, and political powers.

Among the mechanisms of social interaction and conflict resolution are Ritualized Duels. In these confrontations, participants establish beforehand conditions and restrictions formalized through a binding magical contract. The rules may determine the permitted schools, the weapons used, prohibited behaviors, and the criteria for victory. In addition to individual confrontations, these duels may serve as recognized instruments for resolving disputes between magical families and organizations.

The philosophical and religious dimension is shaped by two major schools of thought: Archeism and Uniteism. The former maintains that Existence is founded upon a plurality of Primordial Archetypes, eternal and impersonal principles from which the different manifestations of reality derive. The latter advocates a single conscious Primordial Source, the Creator, from which all Existence proceeds. These interpretations have profoundly shaped human thought and the development of the arcane tradition. The ancient pantheons, once used by various peoples to explain the phenomena of existence, belong to the history of these traditions. With the development of observation and philosophical thought, they gradually lost their function as fundamental explanations of reality. The setting does not adopt great pantheons as the structure of its cosmology, maintaining its focus on these two major interpretations without establishing either as definitive truth.

History is organized into four eras: the Primordial Era, marked by the events of Creation and the Great Schism; the Era of the Immortals, characterized by the rise of immortal entities and the Twilight of the Immortals; the Era of Kings, marked by the development of mortal civilizations and the rise and fall of Aurelion; and the current Era of Mages, defined by the maturation of arcane knowledge and the consolidation of institutions dedicated to the study of magic.

Knowledge about Universum Arcanum is subject to the evidence and interpretations available in each era. Records may contain gaps, traditions may diverge, and theories may be revised. The reality of the universe is not the same as what its inhabitants know or believe they know. This distinction allows the collection itself to reveal different perspectives on the same events and phenomena, without requiring a single or definitive narrative.

## Canonical Works

These bring together the documents that establish the fundamental references of Universum Arcanum. They cover its cosmology, principles, history, geography, entities, and other structural elements, serving as the foundation for the setting's continuity and consistency.

**I. De Natura Existentiae** - *Treatise on the Nature of Existence*: Logos and the nature of Existence; essence and manifestation; origin and fundamental principles of reality; cosmology and structure of the Dominium; classification of the Dominium; Primordial Archetypes and their interpretations; Archeism and Uniteism; nature, origin, and classification of immortal entities; Creation and the Great Schism; Primordial Era; Era of the Immortals and its decline; Era of Kings and the emergence of mortal civilizations; Era of Mages and the rise of arcane knowledge; chronology of the fundamental events of Existence. (work in progress)

**II. Ars Magica** - *Treatise on the Art of Magic*: nature and origin of magic; Harmony, Transformation, Causality, Responsible Freedom, and Inner Flame; magical aptitude and its manifestations; theoretical foundations and arcane laws; magical disciplines and schools; methods of learning and transmitting knowledge; techniques, spells, and incantations; rituals and ceremonies; magical instruments and artifacts; costs, risks, limitations, and consequences of using magic; arcane affinities and incompatibilities; influence of the environment on magical practice; arcane residues and alterations caused by magic; Ritualized Duels and their contracts; traditions, methods, and disagreements among practitioners. (work in progress)

**III. Geographia Arcaniae** - *Treatise on the Geography of Arcania*: position and characteristics of Arcania within the context of the Dominium; structure and configuration of the world; continents, oceans, seas, and major bodies of water; regions, territories, and natural borders; terrain, mountains, plains, deserts, and forests; climate, seasons, and atmospheric phenomena; rivers, lakes, and other water resources; ecosystems, biomes, flora, and fauna; natural species and supernatural creatures; mineral and natural resources; regions with particular magical affinities; areas of instability or arcane anomalies; places of historical or supernatural significance; influence of geographical and environmental characteristics on life and magic. (work in progress)

**IV. Annales Arcaniae** - *Annals of Arcania*: origin and evolution of peoples; species and intelligent groups; cultures, customs, and traditions; migrations and relations between peoples; formation and development of the earliest societies; kingdoms, empires, cities, and other political organizations; dynasties, rulers, and historical figures; wars, conquests, alliances, and treaties; political, social, economic, and military organization; laws, institutions, and systems of government; religions, beliefs, and philosophical practices; languages, writing, literature, arts, and knowledge; relations between magical and non-magical societies; traditional families and arcane lineages; magical schools, their origins, doctrines, and rivalries; institutions and power structures of arcane society; events and transformations that shaped the history of Arcania's civilizations. (work in progress)

## Supplementary Works

These bring together documents that explore specific aspects of the universe in greater depth, expand its context, and present particular perspectives. They include studies, accounts, correspondence, chronicles, and records that enrich the collection without necessarily representing established or consensual knowledge.

### Manuals and Treatises

- **I. Ars Scribendi** - The Art of Writing: calligraphy, textual composition, grammar, rhetoric, literary styles, document structure, formal correspondence, manuscripts, academic records, and conventions of scholarly arcane writing. (work in progress)
  
- **II. Manuale Artificiorum** - Manual of Crafts: techniques, tools, materials, and practices of different crafts, including blacksmithing, carpentry, construction, handicrafts, and other professional activities. (work in progress)
  
- **III. Manuale Adventoris** - Adventurer's Manual: survival, exploration, navigation, tracking, expedition preparation, first aid, and practical experiences of travelers and adventurers. (work in progress)
  
- **IV. De Arte Alchimiae** - On the Art of Alchemy: reagents, preparations, potions, procedures, instruments, known formulas, and alchemical experiments. (work in progress)
  

### Compendiums and Catalogs

- **V. Bestiarium Arcaniae** - Bestiary of Arcania: descriptions, habits, habitats, behaviors, characteristics, and accounts of encounters with creatures known to the author or their sources. (work in progress)
  
- **VI. Herbarium Arcaniae** - Herbarium of Arcania: identification, characteristics, habitats, and known uses of plants, herbs, and fungi, including medicinal, alchemical, and arcane applications. (work in progress)
  
- **VII. Codex Artificiorum** - Codex of Artifacts: records and descriptions of objects, instruments, weapons, relics, and mechanisms of historical, artisanal, or magical interest. (work in progress)
  

### Narrative Works

- **VIII. Fabulae Arcaniae** - Tales of Arcania: collections of fictional stories or stories inspired by events, characters, and everyday experiences in Arcania. (work in progress)
  
- **IX. Legendae Arcaniae** - Legends of Arcania: traditional narratives, regional legends, folk heroes, legendary creatures, and stories passed down through generations. (work in progress)
  
- **X. Chronicae Minoris** - Minor Chronicles: accounts of local events, historical episodes, disputes, discoveries, journeys, and personal experiences recorded by their authors. (work in progress)
  
- **XI. Itineraria Arcaniae** - Journeys through Arcania: travel diaries, exploration accounts, and descriptions of places, peoples, customs, and experiences encountered by travelers. (work in progress)
