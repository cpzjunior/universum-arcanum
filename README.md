# Universum Arcanum

## Introduction

Universum Arcanum is a fictional worldbuilding project in the dark fantasy genre, primarily inspired by a darker interpretation of Harry Potter and by Forgotten Realms. From the former, it draws upon the coexistence of magical and non-magical societies, exploring a reality in which mages wield power, influence, and privileges over the non-magical population. From the latter, it incorporates the breadth of a fantasy setting populated by different cultures, civilizations, creatures, and territories, without adopting a structure of numerous pantheons or excessively extensive lore. The aim is to build a universe rich in possibilities, yet grounded in clear foundations and manageable complexity.

The project's guiding principle is immersion. To achieve this, the documents that make up the universe are presented as works produced by its own inhabitants, situated within specific historical and cultural contexts. A treatise written by an archmage historian, for example, reflects its author's knowledge and perspective, rather than an omniscient view of reality. Thus, the universe is not explained through an external voice, but gradually revealed through its records, traditions, and interpretations.

## Main Characteristics

The cosmology begins with the Logos, understood as the totality of Existence, which encompasses multiple Dominium, each with its own identity, cosmological structure, natural laws, and history. Arcania is the principal domain developed within the project, but it does not represent the entirety of the Logos. Faeris, Ersetu, Aetheris, Cristalis, Nereia, Erebos, and Stygia are among the other known Dominium.

The cosmological structure distinguishes between Existence, essence, and manifestation. Dominium are classified as Mortalis and Immortalis according to the presence of established immortal entities. Immortalis receive an additional classification: Superioris, Inferioris, or Equilibris, according to the predominant disposition of these entities.

Magic is a discipline of knowledge and transformation of reality, founded upon principles such as Harmony, Transformation, Causality, Responsible Freedom, and Inner Flame. Although it exerts a decisive influence on the organization of societies, it is a rare ability: only 1% of the human population manifests magical aptitude. This concentration contributes to social structures in which mages hold privileges, institutional influence, and positions of power inaccessible to most of the population.

Arcane knowledge is preserved and transmitted through Arcane Schools and Families, organizations structured around their own lineages, traditions, and philosophies. Each school possesses its own training methods, techniques, and interpretations of magic. Bonds of belonging, reputation, rivalries, alliances, and succession disputes influence the evolution of these traditions and the distribution of magical knowledge.

Geography also exerts a direct influence on magic. Different territories possess their own affinities, determined by their natural, historical, and supernatural characteristics. These properties can enhance, limit, or alter arcane manifestations. The repeated use of magic may leave energetic residues, cause anomalies, and permanently alter the environment, turning certain regions into strategic resources contested by families, organizations, and political powers.

Among the mechanisms of social interaction and conflict resolution are Ritualized Duels. In these confrontations, participants establish in advance conditions and restrictions formalized through a binding magical contract. The rules may determine the permitted schools, the weapons used, prohibited behaviors, and the criteria for victory. Beyond individual confrontations, these duels may serve as recognized instruments for resolving disputes between magical families and organizations.

The philosophical and religious dimension is shaped by two major schools of thought: Archeism and Uniteism. The former maintains that Existence is founded upon a plurality of Primordial Archetypes, eternal and impersonal principles from which the different manifestations of reality derive. The latter advocates a single conscious Primordial Source, the Creator, from whom all Existence proceeds. These interpretations have profoundly shaped human thought and the development of the arcane tradition. The ancient pantheons, once used by various peoples to explain the phenomena of existence, belong to the history of these traditions. With the development of observation and philosophical thought, they gradually lost their function as fundamental explanations of reality. The setting does not adopt great pantheons as the structure of its cosmology, focusing instead on these two major interpretations without establishing either as definitive truth.

History is organized into four eras: the Primordial Era, marked by the events of Creation and the Great Schism; the Era of the Immortals, characterized by the rise of immortal entities and the Twilight of the Immortals; the Era of Kings, marked by the development of mortal civilizations and the rise and fall of Aurelion; and the current Era of Mages, defined by the maturation of arcane knowledge and the consolidation of institutions devoted to the study of magic.

Knowledge of Universum Arcanum is subject to the evidence and interpretations available in each era. Records may contain gaps, traditions may differ, and theories may be revised. The reality of the universe is not synonymous with what its inhabitants know or believe they know. This distinction allows the body of records itself to reveal different perspectives on the same events and phenomena, without requiring a single, definitive narrative.

## Opera Canonica (Canonical Works)

These comprise the documents that establish the fundamental references of Universum Arcanum. They encompass its cosmology, principles, history, geography, entities, and other structural elements, serving as the foundation for the continuity and consistency of the setting.

### Codex Existentiae (Codex of Existence)

Conceived and written by Archmage Asterion Valerius over the course of more than six decades, and completed in the year 1381 of the Fourth Era, the Codex Existentiae constitutes the most extensive and meticulous treatise ever devoted to the study of Existence in Universum Arcanum. Divided into four books, each dedicated to one of the fundamental pillars of reality, the work addresses the origin and nature of creation, the principles and laws governing magic, the constitution and geography of the realms, as well as the records of events that shaped the ages. Its composition is not limited to the compilation of known knowledge: it seeks to establish an ordered understanding of the Universe itself, its origins, its immutable laws, and the mysteries that remain beyond the comprehension of mortals.

- I. De Natura Existentiae (On the Nature of Existence): Logos and the nature of Existence; essence and manifestation; cosmology and structure of the Dominium; classification of the Dominium; Primordial Archetypes and their interpretations; Archeism and Uniteism; nature, origin, and classification of immortal entities; fundamental principles that sustain Arcania. (work in progress)

- II. De Origine Creationis (On the Origin of Creation): origin of Creation; events of the Primordial Era; Great Schism; principal interpretations concerning the origin of all things; foundations of the different conceptions of Creation and its relationship with Existence. (work in progress)

- III. Ars Magica (The Art of Magic): nature and origin of magic; Harmony, Transformation, Causality, Responsible Freedom, and Inner Flame; magical aptitude and its manifestations; theoretical foundations and arcane laws; magical disciplines and schools; methods of learning and transmission of knowledge; techniques, spells, and incantations; rituals and ceremonies; magical instruments and artifacts; costs, risks, limitations, and consequences of the use of magic; arcane affinities and incompatibilities; influence of the environment on magical practice; arcane residues and alterations caused by magic; Ritualized Duels and their contracts; traditions, methods, and disagreements among practitioners. (work in progress)

- IV. De Societate Arcana (On Arcane Society): origin and development of arcane society; political, social, and institutional organization; Arcane Schools and Families; magical orders, lineages, and traditions; structures of power and authority; hierarchies, offices, and functions; institutions of education and knowledge transmission; relations between arcane organizations; responsibilities, rights, and duties of practitioners; customs, codes of conduct, and traditions of the magical community. (work in progress)

## Opera Complementaria (Complementary Works)

These comprise documents that explore specific aspects of the universe in greater depth, expand its context, and present particular perspectives. They include studies, accounts, correspondence, chronicles, and records that enrich the collection, without necessarily representing established or consensual knowledge.

### Manualia et Tractatus (Manuals and Treatises)

- Ars Scribendi (The Art of Writing): calligraphy, textual composition, grammar, rhetoric, literary styles, document structure, formal correspondence, manuscripts, academic records, and conventions of arcane scholarly writing. (work in progress)

- Manuale Artificiorum (Manual of Crafts): techniques, tools, materials, and practices of different crafts, including blacksmithing, carpentry, construction, handicrafts, and other professional activities. (work in progress)

- Manuale Adventoris (Adventurer's Manual): survival, exploration, navigation, tracking, expedition preparation, first aid, and practical experiences of travelers and adventurers. (work in progress)

- De Arte Alchimiae (On the Art of Alchemy): reagents, preparations, potions, procedures, instruments, known formulas, and alchemical experiments. (work in progress)

### Compendia et Catalogi (Compendiums and Catalogs)

- Bestiarium Arcaniae (Bestiary of Arcania): descriptions, habits, habitats, behaviors, characteristics, and accounts of encounters with creatures known to the author or their sources. (work in progress)

- Herbarium Arcaniae (Herbarium of Arcania): identification, characteristics, habitats, and known uses of plants, herbs, and fungi, including medicinal, alchemical, and arcane applications. (work in progress)

- Codex Artificiorum (Codex of Artifacts): records and descriptions of objects, instruments, weapons, relics, and mechanisms of historical, artisanal, or magical significance. (work in progress)

### Opera Narrativa (Narrative Works)

- Fabulae Arcaniae (Tales of Arcania): collections of fictional stories or stories inspired by events, characters, and everyday experiences in Arcania. (work in progress)

- Legendae Arcaniae (Legends of Arcania): traditional narratives, regional legends, folk heroes, legendary creatures, and stories passed down through generations. (work in progress)

- Chronicae Minoris (Minor Chronicles): accounts of local events, historical episodes, disputes, discoveries, journeys, and personal experiences recorded by their authors. (work in progress)

- Itineraria Arcaniae (Journeys Through Arcania): travel diaries, exploration accounts, and descriptions of places, peoples, customs, and experiences encountered by travelers. (work in progress)
